Privacy Policy — Sushi Sort: Kaiten Bar

Effective date: 2026-11-09

This policy explains what information the game Sushi Sort: Kaiten Bar (the "Game") collects, why, who receives it and the choices you have. The Game is published by Kaiten Games ("we", "us"). Contact: alexandre.zanelatto@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • No account or login. We do not ask for your name, email, phone or address.
  • Your game progress stays on your device.
  • We use Google Firebase to understand how the Game is played and to fix crashes.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details.
  • Ads (when shown) come from Google AdMob and, later, its mediation partners; personalized ads only with your consent where the law requires it.
  • You can ask us to delete your data using the Player ID shown in the Game's Settings.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Stored only on your device

Game progress (levels, coins, stars, lives, boosters, restaurant decorations), settings (sound, vibration) and a random Player ID created the first time you open the Game. Uninstalling the Game deletes this data.

2.2 Usage and device data (Google Firebase Analytics)

Game events (for example: level started or finished, boosters used, purchases offered), an app instance identifier, your Player ID, device model, operating system and version, app version, language, and approximate country/region derived from your IP address. Firebase Analytics in the Game does not collect your device's advertising ID.

2.3 Crash reports (Google Firebase Crashlytics)

When the Game crashes or hits an error: technical details of the error, device state, an installation identifier and your Player ID.

2.4 Game settings (Google Firebase Remote Config)

An installation identifier used to deliver game settings (for example prices of in-game items).

2.5 Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We (through our purchase service, RevenueCat) receive the purchase record: product, date, country, transaction identifiers and an anonymous app user identifier, so we can deliver what you bought and restore it. We never receive card or bank details.

2.6 Advertising (when ads are enabled)

Our ad partner Google (AdMob) and the ad networks it mediates for us (currently: none yet; ads come only from Google AdMob) may collect your device's advertising ID (if you allow it), IP address, device and network information, approximate location derived from the IP address, and how you interact with ads, in order to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, with your consent where required, personalize them.

4. Who receives your data

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share data only with providers that run the Game for us or show ads:

  • Google (Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) — privacy policy, Firebase privacy
  • RevenueCat (purchases) — privacy policy
  • Google (AdMob ads) — how Google uses data in ads, privacy policy
  • Ad networks used through Google AdMob mediation: none yet; this list is updated before any is enabled
  • Google Play / Apple (payments) under their own policies

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Sharing data with ad networks for personalized advertising may be considered "sharing" or a "sale" under some US state laws; you can opt out as described below.

5. How long we keep it

  • Analytics events: up to 14 months.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • Purchase records: as long as required by tax and consumer law.
  • Data on your device: until you uninstall the Game.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Privacy choices: where the law requires consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland), the Game asks before showing ads, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, withdrawal of consent (GDPR, UK GDPR, LGPD) and access, deletion and opt-out of sale/sharing (US state laws such as CCPA/CPRA): email alexandre.zanelatto@gmail.com with your Player ID (Settings in the Game). We answer within 15 days (Brazil) or 30 days (other places). You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD).

7. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All data sent by the Game travels encrypted (HTTPS). Access to the analytics and crash consoles is restricted to the development team.

9. Changes

We will update this page and its effective date when this policy changes, and tell you in the Game when the change is significant.

10. Contact

Kaiten Games — Brazil — alexandre.zanelatto@gmail.com. This email is also the channel for data subject requests under the LGPD.
