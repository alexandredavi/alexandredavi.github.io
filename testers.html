Become a tester — Sushi Sort: Kaiten Bar

Sushi Sort is a cozy puzzle: sort the sushi plates on the conveyor belt and serve every customer. Before it reaches everyone on Google Play, it needs a small group of players to try it for 14 days. It takes a few minutes a day, and you play before anyone else.

You need an Android phone and the Google account you use in the Play Store. iPhones can't join this test.

How to join

  1. Join the testers group with the same Google account you use in the Play Store: groups.google.com/g/kaiten-testers → Join group.
  2. Open the test link and tap Become a tester: play.google.com/apps/testing/com.kaitengames.sushisort.
  3. Install the game from the Play Store: Sushi Sort: Kaiten Bar. It may take a few minutes after step 2 to show up.

Steps 2 and 3 work once the test opens. If you join the group before that, we'll send you a message when it starts.

During the 14 days

  • Keep the game installed and stay in the test until the end: Google counts the testers every day.
  • Play a few levels on different days. There's no need to play a lot, but play for real.
  • Updates will arrive through the Play Store with fixes based on what testers tell us.
  • The game shows test ads (labeled "Test Ad") at the same moments and as often as the final version will. No need to tap them; tell us in the form if they show up too often or at a bad moment.

Tell us what you think

Was it fun? Where did you get stuck? Did anything break? Fill in the feedback form (2 minutes; answer as many times as you like during the 14 days, no email or sign-in). Your Player ID (Game → Settings) helps us find a bug you saw.

Prefer email? alexandre.zanelatto@gmail.com

Thank you

Testers who stay until the end get a thank-you in the game's credits (only if you want it, with the name you choose).

What the game collects is in the Privacy Policy. Testing is free and you can leave at any time, from the test link or the group.
